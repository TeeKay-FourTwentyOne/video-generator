# Pierrot — original Hellzapoppin character

Stills of the sign-holding Pierrot from **Hellzapoppin’ No. 5 — The Pepper’s Ghost**. The original character reference is byte-identical to the reference used to create the lead in **Still Juggling**.

- `00_contact_sheet.jpg`: overview of the 12 full images.
- `01_original_reference.png`: unchanged original character reference; the clearest image of her original design.
- `02`–`12`: native-resolution PNG frames from the original HELL, POP, and REMEMBER NOTHING source clips.
- `portraits/`: six face-and-shoulder detail crops, kept at their source pixel resolution.
- `00_portrait_sheet.jpg`: overview of those detail crops.
- `sources.json`: exact relative source paths, frame numbers, timestamps, crop coordinates, dimensions, and SHA-256 hashes.

The source clips are the untrimmed Hellzapoppin production shots, so some selected moments may extend beyond the trims in the final edit. All images come from the original photoreal production; none come from Still Juggling or the earlier painterly draft. The feather headdress and the presence or absence of the black ruff reflect the original shots.

For this original-stills collection, no new images were generated and no source images were retouched or upscaled. The reference retains its original dimensions; video stills are 720 × 1280. Preview sheets alone are scaled for browsing.

## New generated variations

[generated-poses-v1/](generated-poses-v1/README.md) contains eight new image-generator variations in different poses and locations, with a contact sheet and the complete prompt set. These are separate from the original Hellzapoppin stills indexed below.
