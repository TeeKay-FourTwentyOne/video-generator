# Pierrot pose and location variations — prompts

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. Eight independent generations, each referencing the original image and its unchanged portrait crop.

Reference images, relative to this folder:
- `../01_original_reference.png`
- `../portraits/01_original_reference_portrait.png`

Each final prompt is the following common block, two newline characters, then the corresponding scene block. Transparent background: false.

## Common identity and style block

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: a new cinematic character reference still for the existing fictional Pierrot.
Input image 1 is the authoritative character identity and costume reference. Input image 2 is an unaltered close crop of exactly that same reference, provided only for precise facial identity. Create a NEW pose and location of this SAME woman, not a lookalike or generic clown. Preserve her facial proportions, eye shape, subtle natural skin texture under makeup, slender build, chalk-white face paint, precisely one small black tear below each eye, fine dark brows and eyeliner, small glossy red bow-shaped lips, and fitted black satin skullcap concealing her hair. Match the original youthful adult face and sly understated character. White satin loose long-sleeved Pierrot blouse with three large WHITE fabric pompoms down the front and smaller white sleeve pompoms, matching full white satin trousers gathered at ankles, simple black ballet slippers when feet are visible. Keep the plain collar of the original reference, without adding a ruff or feather headdress.
Style: lifelike cinematic still with the original's rich 1950s Technicolor period-film feeling, convincing woven satin, real practical architecture and lighting, delicate film grain, restrained color. Photographic realism, not illustration, painting, doll or 3D cartoon. Her face must be clearly readable and faithful to the reference.
Constraints: exactly one person and one single image, no montage, no text, no signage, no letters, no watermark, no juggling balls, no placard. Anatomically natural hands and limbs. Change pose, framing and environment as requested while preserving identity and costume. Vertical 9:16 composition.
```
