# Direction and process field notes

Boundaries: `AGENTS.md`. Workflow: `docs/production.md`.

### Autonomous build doctrine
One planned generation per shot; the keep, edit-around, flag ladder; no
autonomous rerolls. A retake is justified only by a terminal failure (filtered,
empty) or a defect that leaves nothing usable, and it changes what caused the
defect. Everything else goes to the director as a decision with timestamps and
options, in the workspace plan and the delivery notes.
Source: claude-code · 2026-08 to 2026-10

### Accept and adapt
"Good enough" is the default. Rescue downstream (trim, sound effect, programmatic
patch) before regenerating. Hold the plan loosely; let the footage set the cut.
Source: claude-code · 2026-08

### Show imperfect clips
The director decides keep, edit or reroll. Flagged takes are delivered with the
flag, the window and the recommended treatment.
Source: claude-code · 2026-08

### Composition latitude inside an approved direction
Make per-shot framing and staging calls without asking. Piece-defining structural
choices (a new scene, a changed ending, a recast) are explicit check-ins before
building, never asides.
Source: claude-code · 2026-08 to 2026-09

### Continuous joins are first-class deliverables
Plan a seamless join structurally, splice with a fine scan, and QA each join
before calling the piece done.
Source: claude-code · Personal Best · 2026-08

### Freshness over the judge panel
Concept judges repeat the last one or two shipped pieces; override for
freshness while the director keeps taste authority.
Source: claude-code · 2026-09

### Budget expectations
Every attempt bills; plan about 30% over clip arithmetic; QA, images and storage
share the same pot and are reconciled from saved usage. Two independent drafts
of a 33-second Quality silent vertical with Nano anchors and local sound came in
at about $11 to $12 all-in, roughly 68% of generated seconds used.
Source: claude-code · Borrowed Light · 2026-10-03

### Record decisions where the next session will find them
The workspace plan keeps the blocking board, the per-shot decision table, the
budget table and the decision queue; `qa/README.md` keeps the gate record. A
brief in `briefs/` gets a short dated section per draft.
Source: claude-code · Borrowed Light · 2026-10-03

### Series conventions live with their briefs
Hand-painted banners, a photoreal register, a recurring host: those belong to
the series brief or `series/`, not to these notes.
Source: claude-code · Hellzapoppin · 2026-05

### Budget expectations, second data point
A 38.6-second vertical with seven Quality takes (two with native audio), one retake,
nine Nano anchors (two rejected), 20 Claude QA calls and a $0.30 reserve committed
$16.41 of $20 (list-price and saved-usage estimates): Veo $14.40, images $1.25,
QA $0.46. About 64% of generated seconds reached the cut.
Source: claude-code · Washing Day · 2026-10-03

### Taste and conviction are expected, not tolerated
The director's note after October: the working rules read as harsh for an artistic
project. They guard money, privacy and continuity; they do not set the register of
the work. Make craft choices with a point of view, let the returned material
change the plan, and offer ideas the brief did not ask for, marked as yours. Keep
the structural check-ins (what the piece is, spend, publication).
**Apply:** when two choices are both acceptable, take the one you would defend and
say why in the delivery; an unasked-for coda that costs nothing is a gift, not a liberty.
Source: director · October · 2026-10-08
