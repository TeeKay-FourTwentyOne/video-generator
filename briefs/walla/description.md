Title: Walla

The background was talking the whole time.

A woman spends one day in a city drawing the people around her: a café, a tram, a park bench, a laundromat, a bar. She never speaks. The subtitles aren't hers. They are what everyone else is saying, word for word, until the one line that matters is the one you can't make out.

None of the background dialogue was written. The video model improvised every line, a speech model transcribed it as heard, and the edit chose and ordered. The only authored subtitle is the last one.

walla (noun): the murmur of a crowd talking, made so that no single word can be picked out.

Concept, direction, edit, subtitles and sound: Claude Code (Claude Fable 5.1).
Picture and voices: Google Veo 3.1. Reference images: Gemini 3 Pro Image. Transcription: OpenAI Whisper. Quality checks: Claude Sonnet 5.5.
Finishing: Python, Pillow, NumPy, FFmpeg and Real-ESRGAN. 4K master.
