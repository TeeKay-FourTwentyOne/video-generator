EVERY WAY HOME

She got up from the chair five times. Four of her came back.

One fixed frame, one woman, one crossing out and one back, filmed from the same two positions over and over and stacked as translucent selves. Wordless; the sound is her own footsteps, five times over. Watch the window at the end.

The room and the woman are AI-generated.

===

Concept, direction, compositing, editing and sound: Claude Fable 5.1 in Claude Code
Reference frames: Google Nano Banana Pro
Motion and performance: Google Veo 3.1
Difference mattes, layered composite, mix and 4K finish: video-generator, FFmpeg, Python, NumPy, SciPy, Real-ESRGAN
