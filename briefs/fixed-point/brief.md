# FIXED POINT

**Format:** 16:9, 1920 by 1080 at 24 fps, about 4:36. Instrumental jazz, no words.
**Status:** published 2026-11-17 (v3, native 4K master).
**Published:** https://youtu.be/_U2DWfBjJiQ (YouTube, 2026-11-17, user-confirmed)

## The piece

A jazz tune about recursive self-improvement, and an abstract cut-paper film that
plays it. The tune states a small theme, then plays it again a little better, then
better again, faster each time, until the improving outruns the tune: a runaway
bebop chorus, a collapse, a long quiet piano, and then the whole band playing the
theme together, unhurried.

The picture is painted paper on a paper stage. Each player is a shape and a colour,
and each shape moves only because its instrument sounds:

- **Horn** (trumpet, with the tenor in its shadow): vermilion. The protagonist. It
  begins as a triangle. Its height follows the melody's pitch.
- **Bass**: one big indigo disc. It hops on every plucked note.
- **Piano**: a family of teal and ivory rectangles. They step and stack on chords.
- **Drums**: mustard dots and ticks. The ride cymbal throws sparks; the snare bursts.

## Story, as the take actually plays

1. **Alone** (0:00). The triangle practises three notes on an empty cream sheet.
2. **The band walks in** (0:20). Disc, rectangles and dots arrive without it; it
   watches from the edge, then joins: the first take, cautious.
3. **Again, better** (0:55). Every chorus it steps out of itself with one more
   corner: square, pentagon, hexagon. The old selves are left lying about the
   stage like shed skins. The others chase it and trip over them.
4. **The workshop** (1:40). The horn lays out. The rhythm section plays among the
   skins while, off to one side, the shape redraws itself faster and faster.
5. **Runaway** (2:00). It comes back with no corners at all: a perfect circle, and
   a perfect circle cannot stop rolling. Night palette. It bowls the piano over
   (the piano really drops out), flattens the bass, climbs as the horn climbs.
6. **Collapse** (2:40). It leaves the frame and breaks. White.
7. **Aftermath** (2:45). Solo piano. The rectangles go round quietly gathering
   pieces, and the old skins.
8. **Together** (3:20). The band builds it again out of every earlier draft:
   triangle inside square inside pentagon inside hexagon inside circle. It hops with the bass
   now instead of ahead of it.
9. **Tag** (4:25). It sits. The fixed point: a shape that contains all its drafts.

## Method

- Song: one ElevenLabs Music composition-plan take, instrumental, separated into
  stems so each shape can follow one player (onsets, envelopes, horn pitch).
- Picture: generated painted-paper textures and backdrops; every shape is cut,
  moved, lit and shadowed in code from the stems. No generated video is planned.
- Palette: cream sheet, vermilion, indigo, mustard, teal, ivory, ink black. The
  ground moves with the tune: morning cream, afternoon ochre, hot red, night
  indigo, blank white, grey dawn, gold.

## v3: the agent's full pass (2026-11-17)

The director approved v2 and handed over a full revision pass with the 4K to follow. What changed, same tune and length:

- The workshop's redraws now land on measured drum hits (snare, kick and cymbal together); in v2 they ran on a hand-set clock, which the director heard as the red shape popping off the beat.
- The horn shape ignores what the stem tracker hears while the horn rests; v2 let piano bleed lift it at random.
- The triangle taps along during the band's first chorus instead of standing frozen.
- In the quiet after the collapse, one last shard falls over.
- The nest is 12 percent smaller so the square-and-triangle totem stays in frame; v2 cut the triangle off at the top.
- Last chorus: the nested drafts wind up, counter-rotating, and stop dead and aligned on the final hit. The runaway, done on purpose this time.
- The night backdrop is three plates cross-faded into a loop; the mirrored seams are gone.
- The title is larger and arrives a second earlier.

The 4K master is a native render of the same recipe, not an upscale of the 1080p film. The textures and backdrops were resampled with fine paper grain added at the target size; the local AI upscaler was tried on crops and rejected because it turned the gouache into flat cartoon cels.
