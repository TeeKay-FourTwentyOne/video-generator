FIXED POINT

A triangle hears a band and decides to get better. Then it gets better at getting better.

A four-and-a-half-minute cut-paper jazz film about recursive self-improvement. The horn is the red shape, the bass is the blue circle, the piano is the five keys and the drums are the gold dots; every move on screen is driven by that player's part in the tune. Instrumental, no words. Watch what is inside the circle at the end.

===

Concept, tune plan, choreography, renderer and editing: Claude Fable 5.2 in Claude Code
Music: ElevenLabs Music, one take separated into stems
Paper textures and backdrops: Google Nano Banana Pro
Animation and native 4K render: Python, NumPy, SciPy, OpenCV, FFmpeg
