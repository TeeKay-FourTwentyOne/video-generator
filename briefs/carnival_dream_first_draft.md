projected light. Their first dance transforms the theatre into a carnival;
the light carries her costume's colors away when it leaves. She gathers paper
stars to prolong it, discovers the rotating lantern behind the scenery, and
deliberately steps back into its beam. The ending preserves the choice's
ambiguity: the pleasure is real even when its mechanism is visible.
