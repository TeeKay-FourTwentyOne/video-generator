## 1. Concept

A string of auto-reply messages, set as clean type over people doing gloriously unproductive things. The machine has the spreadsheet. The humans are out, and they are bad at what they are doing, and they have never been happier.

The optimistic case is not that we get more done. It is that we get to be worse at more things, on purpose.

## 2. Format

- 1080×1920 (9:16), 24 fps. Target about 30 s.
- Bright, flat daylight; deadpan, centred, locked-off frames. Six or seven shots of three to four seconds, hard cuts on the beat.
- Type is the voice: one sans face, set large in the top and middle of the frame, clear of the Shorts interface zone. No narration.
- Music: one cheerful, slightly incompetent solo cello, a beginner's étude that gets a little better by the end and no more than a little.

## 4. Agent's choices

- Incompetence is the joke and the thesis: every activity is done badly and with total commitment. Nobody is shown being improved.
- No robot, no screen, no interface anywhere in the picture. The machine exists only as the reason nobody is at their desk.
- The cello on the soundtrack is the man in card 3.

## 5. Risks

- It lives on the writing; a flat card kills a four-second shot. Read the cards aloud against a metronome before planning shots.
- Smugness. The people must be ordinary and the pleasures small; no beaches, no luxury.
- The lightest of the three ideas from this brainstorm. Its job is charm.

## 6. Build notes (2026-11-23)

The director approved the build, the subject-line opening and the returning dog. The card pass produced the working set below; section 3 stays as the original intent.

| # | Card | Picture as shot |

| 1 | *Automatic reply:* **Out of office**, then *I am away from my desk.* | The chair turns and settles facing the room. |
| 2 | *The machine has the spreadsheet.* then *I have this cloud. I believe it is a horse.* | She points, reconsiders, points again. The cloud is no shape at all. |
| 3 | *I am learning the cello.* then *Badly.* | The dog gets up and leaves; he does not notice. |
| 4 | *For urgent matters, please consider whether they are.* | The boat hook does not reach. The boat drifts off. |
| 5 | *I have limited access to email* then *and unlimited access to this.* | The dough strings up and sags. |
| 6 | *I will respond upon my return.* then *I will not be returning.* | The long table. The cello man is there; the dog is asleep underneath. |
| 7 | *Warm regards,* (unsigned) | The chair, stopped, with a plant on it. |

- Type: Helvetica Neue, near-black, set left at the top of the frame, appearing on the cut; second lines land on the cello's phrase starts.
- Music: a generated solo-cello study, damaged locally into a beginner's performance (a false start, sour notes and hesitations that thin out, a last note he finds). The cuts sit on its phrases; the last note lands on the plant.
- Two local picture adjustments keep the type on clear ground: the dough shot is mirrored and the table shot is enlarged slightly from its bottom edge.
