## Dialogue and voices

Try native Veo audio for the Captain, Reptile Commander and Tactical Officer. [Canonical voice blocks](voices.json) must appear verbatim in every relevant generation prompt. Emotional direction follows the stable voice identity; it does not replace it. The Captain's quiet private delivery in the final scene is the same voice as his command delivery.

Ship/Computer and Probe retain the separate voice workflow. The Ship is the same voice in space and in the quarters; Probe is a drier register of that intelligence. The Reptile's underlying relationship to the Ship voice is an audition target, not a claim of exact cross-provider voice matching.

Use no generated music in dialogue takes. Build the score separately so the hard music cuts and the exterior quiet stay controllable. Review exact words, speaker attribution, lip sync and cross-shot voice continuity before accepting a performance. Longer scripted scenes will require intentional coverage within the maintained provider limits.

## Visible phenomenon

The director requested a visible phenomenon and delegated its design. Agent choice: a narrow, folded ribbon of translucent silver-blue light with a faint violet edge. A fine luminous crease and soft internal shimmer make it readable against black space without turning it into a portal, explosion or threat.

S06 and S08 share the same ribbon, probe and ship design. The ribbon is the quiet object of the real science; the line “Beautiful” remains understated. S04 uses a different outward camera angle with the phenomenon outside the frame, preserving the early empty-space reveal.

The spoken twenty-three-millisecond measurement is a story detail. The eventual visual shimmer should be slow and soft; do not turn it into a rapid full-frame strobe.
