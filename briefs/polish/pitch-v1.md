# POLISH

9:16 · 32 seconds · Working title

A messaging window. Someone asks, “Are you okay?” The reply being typed is honest,
hesitant. Writing assistance keeps improving it: “I’m struggling” becomes “Doing
well.” “I need someone to talk to” becomes “Let’s catch up sometime.” Each
correction arrives with a pleasant little sparkle.

Finally: “Please stop.” It becomes a thumbs-up and sends.

**The reply: “Good. I was worried about you.”**

Almost entirely text, but with a very human rhythm in the typing and deletions.
