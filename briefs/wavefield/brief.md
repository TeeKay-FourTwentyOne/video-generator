# Wavefield

A 24-second portrait abstract film. One continuous approach through a folded luminous structure in overlapping positions. Computed complex wave amplitudes produce interference, while paired generated material studies inform its surface.

Six clicks occur at 2, 6, 10, 14, 18 and 22 seconds. Their spacing stays fixed while clarity, presence and structural disturbance grow. The detector sounds like a short mechanical clock tick. At the final click the structure disappears; a tiny light remains in near-silence and expands rapidly to full white. End on white.

The director authorized independent selection through six paired milestones. Every selected image remains linked to its parent and both candidates are retained. Native format is 1080 by 1920, 30 fps, 720 frames.

This is an artistic wave-field construction inspired by objective-collapse questions. It does not implement GRW, CSL or Diosi-Penrose dynamics and makes no experimental claim. The calculation, numerical verification and artistic remapping are documented separately.

Production sources, original generations, numerical arrays, audio stems, render recipe and review evidence remain in the private versioned workspace.

The director's second pass keeps the piece and asks for a shorter distant opening,
an earlier approach, and an extreme close-up of one intersection before the final
click. Surrounding strands progressively gather into that point and disappear.
Colored ripples make each detector disturbance more visible. The revision uses
the existing material studies and preserves the six click times and soundtrack.

The third pass makes the opening push-in about one third slower. It eases back
onto the second draft's camera path just after eight seconds, preserving the
later convergence, final close-up and soundtrack.

The fourth pass changes sound only: short, sharp detector ticks replace the
booming hits. The low resonator is removed and the reflections shortened, becoming
fully dry at the final click. Picture, click timing and quiet field air are retained.

The director authorized a 4K upscale of v4. The finishing master uses a local
2160 by 3840 resize, the same 720-frame edit and the exact v4 soundtrack.

The director subsequently supplied the [publication URL](https://youtube.com/shorts/ew_MaUnZ9tI)
and requested closeout. The [release and restoration notes](closeout.md) identify
the retained master and the dependencies of earlier revisions.
