SECOND SPEAKER

She is the last person who speaks her language. Then there are two.

A grandmother teaches a small machine the word for the light before evening, and corrects it until it gets it right. The subtitles arrive only as the machine understands her.

The language was invented for this film. It is not a real language and does not stand for any real people or place.
