# FALSE COLOR — TOGETHER

A companion to *False Color — Green*: the viewer experiences an illusion before
the film exposes the unchanged geometry underneath it. The connecting idea is
company: identical circles appear unequal among different neighbours; independent
straight motions become a rolling wheel when seen together.

## Original production brief

Working title during production: **Together**. Portrait 9:16, 38 seconds,
1080 × 1920 at 60 fps.
All picture, typography and sound are constructed locally. The exact stimuli are
the artwork; photographic decoration would weaken the comparison.

| Time | Beat | Picture and sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | S01: Which blue is bigger? | Two identical blue discs, one among small ivory discs and one among large ones. A sparse repeating glass-note motif. |
| 4–9 s | S02: Company changes | The surrounding discs exchange sizes while the blue targets remain pixel-identical. The musical harmony changes beneath the same motif. |
| 9–14 s | S03: Show the equality | Surrounds dissolve. Matching guides appear; the two blue discs meet and coincide. The sound pares down to one clear tone. |
| 14–20 s | S04: A wheel emerges | The disc becomes one of eight dots in Tusi motion. Establish the rolling gestalt, then highlight one dot. |
| 20–27 s | S05: Expose the paths | First reveal the blue dot's straight track, then all eight diameters. Continuous motion across the reveal. |
| 27–35 s | S06: Together again | Remove the guides and equalize the dots' colours. The wheel returns. Music opens into a rounded, repeating arpeggio. |
| 35–38 s | S07: Title | The moving dots fade around TOGETHER. A single resolving chord. |

## Creative choices

The pairing, blue/ivory palette, merge transition and musical construction are
agent proposals. No fast flashing, narrow moiré gratings, prolonged fixation or
brightness instruction is needed. Text stays above the stimulus and inside the
portrait platform safe area. No caption claims that the collective wheel is
physically impossible: each individual dot follows a straight line, while its
collective motion supports a real rolling-wheel interpretation.

## Basis and attribution

- [Ebbinghaus size-context demonstration](https://michaelbach.de/ot/cog-Ebbinghaus/),
  Michael Bach. The targets remain exactly equal; only their surroundings change.
- [Quantifying the Ebbinghaus figure effect](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4631937/).
  Target size, inducer size and spacing all matter; effect strength still requires
  a viewer check on the intended display.
- [Tusi or not Tusi](https://illusionoftheyear.com/2013/05/tusi-or-not-tusi/),
  Arthur Shapiro and Alex Rose-Henig, 2013.
- [Tusi motion demonstration](https://michaelbach.de/ot/mot-Tusi/index.html),
  Michael Bach. Straight individual trajectories and a rotating collective
  percept are compatible descriptions, not contradictory physical claims.
