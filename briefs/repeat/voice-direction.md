Use the following direction verbatim in every dialogue generation:

> A single adult woman with a natural medium-low speaking register, soft slightly
> dry texture, clear neutral American English, unhurried conversational delivery,
> lightly breathy onset but fully voiced words. Ordinary, unperformed and intimate;
> no announcer cadence, no sing-song inflection, no robot effect, no accent changes.
> Use that exact voice for all spoken lines. Clean close microphone, very quiet
> white-room air, no music, no other people or voices, no computer speech, no echo
> playback.

The three on-camera samples are “The room is quiet,” “I can hear you,” and “It’s
good to be here.” The third take also supplies a separate “This is my voice”
utterance for the takeover. That utterance is used as disembodied playback over
the listening performance, not as an additional sample read on camera.

Every repeat uses its own original recorded utterance. Process those samples
locally: heavily distorted, lightly artificial, then unchanged. The eventual
stinger reuses the clean third sample. Do not generate substitute playback voices.

Record the source hash and sample/frame ranges for each utterance and repeat.

Veo produced the same voice. Preserve normal-speed picture and synchronized
native speech. Keep technical checks distinct from listening and director review.
