## Original production intent

9:16 · 30–40 seconds maximum · Production brief 2026-11-15

Voice setup: mostly a fixed, straight-on close-up of one person in a
non-descript sterile white room. Plain clothes, soft flat white light, no set
dressing. A small waveform sits below their face. The interface is quiet and
legible; the human face and the sound carry the film.

Three short sample sentences, each followed by its own playback:

1. **“The room is quiet.”** The repeat is heavily distorted, broken and barely
   human. Their expression registers a small uncertainty.
2. **“I can hear you.”** The repeat is much closer, with only a thin artificial
   edge. They listen more carefully.
3. **“It’s good to be here.”** The repeat is the exact clean recording. A brief
   moment of recognition before the system takes over.

Keep the lines and transitions short. Construct the distortion and waveform
locally from the recorded speech so the progression is deliberate and the third
repeat is literally their voice. The sample wording is the agent's execution of
the director's three-line setup.

The final prompt appears: “This is my voice.” Before the person can read it, the
playback says it for them. They try to speak; their microphone switches off. Their
own voice continues calmly.

**“This is my voice.” Green check: “Ready to use.”**

The face remains on screen, listening. Then the meaning lands: a caught breath,
eyes widening, a small shake of the head. They try again to speak and lean toward
us, frightened and increasingly agitated. Their own voice carries on without
them. Hold long enough to see that they understand what has happened.

**Hard cut to black. A short, silent pause.**

Snap back to the same person, straight on, close to the lens. They pound their
closed fist twice against an invisible barrier exactly at the picture plane. The
knuckles stop against it, recoil, and strike again: it looks as though they are
pounding on our screen, trying to get out. Keep the face visible behind the fist,
eyes urgently meeting ours. The camera stays fixed; the barrier has no visible
edge, reflection or crack.

Over this, the clean recording of their third sample plays again, warmly and
with easy pleasure:

**“It’s good to be here.”**

Their mouth does not deliver the line. Two muffled impacts sit beneath the clean,
untroubled voice. The stinger lasts about two seconds. Cut to black again as the
line ends.

## Ending direction

The director's revision establishes fear or agitation while the person listens,
then a brief stinger after black: pleasant speech against frantic fist contact,
seen squarely from the viewer's side of an invisible barrier.

The specific line, two impacts and muffled contact sound are the agent's proposed
execution. Keep the stolen voice identical to the samples and casually pleasant.
The human performance supplies the panic. Leave a small pocket of silence before
the stinger; the first impact brings the picture back. Preserve the same person,
wardrobe, room and frontal axis so the stinger reads as the fate of the person we
have been watching.

Working total: about 31–33 seconds, including the black pause and stinger; never
more than 40 seconds. Allow the final prompt and frightened listening to gather
over about six to eight seconds, then roughly three quarters of a second of
black and two to three seconds for the stinger. The exact lengths follow the
performances. These are planning targets, not a rendered timeline.
